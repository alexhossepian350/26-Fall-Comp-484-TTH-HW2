header (role="banner") - the top block of the page, holds the h1, the h2 and the nav
nav (role="navigation") - the site menu, the list of links to the other 6 pages
main (role="main") - wraps the content so a screen reader can skip past the header and menu
article - the actual page content, marked this way since it could be by istelf
header (the second one, inside the article) - the intro, heading, and first paragraph
section - each of the three main topics under the article
aside (role="complementary") - the W3C Warning box, extra info that isn't required to understand the section it sits in
footer (role="contentinfo") - bottom of the page with the CSUN address
